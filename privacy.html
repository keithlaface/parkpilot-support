Privacy Policy

Park Pilot is built to keep your data on your device.

What the app uses

  • Location: to show where you are in the park and calculate walking directions to your next stop. Used only on your device; never transmitted to us or anyone else.
  • Calendar: only if you choose to import dining reservations or Lightning Lane times. Stays on your device.
  • Contacts: only if you choose a person with Apple's system contact picker. Park Pilot receives only the contact you select and uses the selected name, birthday-derived age, and photo to create a party member on your device.
  • Photos: only if you choose an image with Apple's system photo picker. Park Pilot receives only the image you select, resizes it for the party-member profile, and stores it on your device.
  • Wait times: the app requests public ride wait times from queue-times.com. These requests contain only a park identifier and no personal information about you.
  • Weather: provided by Apple WeatherKit for the park you select.
  • Settings: your notification preferences are stored on your device.

What we collect

Nothing leaves your device. Park Pilot has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We do not receive, collect, sell, or share your personal information. Information you enter or select is stored only on your device as part of your saved trip.

Storage, deletion, and access

  • Party-member details and selected photos remain on your device for as long as that member remains in the saved trip.
  • You can change or remove a member's photo from Edit Party Member, or delete the party member from the party list. Removing Park Pilot deletes its locally stored app data.
  • The Contacts and Photos pickers do not give Park Pilot ongoing access to either library. They provide only the contact or image you select, so there is no continuing Contacts or Photos permission to revoke.
  • You can revoke Location or Calendar access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security.

Children

Park Pilot does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
