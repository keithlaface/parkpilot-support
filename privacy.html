Privacy Policy

Park Pilot is built to keep your data on your device.

What the app uses

  • Location: to show where you are in the park and calculate walking directions to your next stop. Park Pilot does not send your live location to its own servers or to Queue-Times.
  • Calendar: only if you choose to import plans for your selected park day. Imported events become drafts that you review before saving. This information stays on your device.
  • Pasted text: only if you paste a confirmation or itinerary. Park Pilot extracts plan names and times on your device. The pasted text is not uploaded or retained after processing; only plans you review and add are saved.
  • Contacts: only if you choose a person with Apple's system contact picker. Park Pilot receives only the contact you select and uses the selected name, birthday-derived age, and photo to create a party member on your device.
  • Photos: only if you choose an image with Apple's system photo picker. A party-member photo is resized and stored on your device. A reservation screenshot or photo is read with Apple's on-device text recognition; that source image is not retained after processing, and only plans you review and add are saved.
  • Wait times: the app sends the selected provider park identifier to Queue-Times.com. It does not send your live location or any party, reservation, contact, photo, or pasted-text content. Like any internet request, Queue-Times and its network providers can receive the request's IP address, time, and standard client metadata. Queue-Times' published privacy policy describes IP and browser-request logging and names analytics, advertising, and security providers for its website, but it does not say which practices apply to its app-facing API or how long API request metadata is retained. Park Pilot will not finalize its App Store privacy answers until that API-specific handling is confirmed.
  • Weather: the app sends the selected park's public coordinates to Apple WeatherKit to request current conditions. It does not send your live location.

What we collect

Park Pilot has no backend user accounts, custom analytics, advertising, third-party tracking code, device identifier, or partner reporting feed. Park Pilot's developer does not operate a server that receives your trip or usage data and does not sell your personal information. Information you enter or select is stored only on your device as part of your saved trip. The app intentionally sends only a provider park identifier to Queue-Times or the selected park's public coordinates to Apple Weather; the standard request metadata visible to those services is described above.

Apple may provide the developer with aggregate App Store usage metrics and crash or diagnostic reports from people who chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS. Apple controls that system-level choice and its privacy protections. Park Pilot does not add reservation text, screenshots, party details, contact selections, photos, precise location, or route history to analytics or diagnostics, and we do not share Apple's reports with partners. You can manage Apple's choice in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Storage, deletion, and access

  • Party-member details and selected photos remain on your device for as long as that member remains in the saved trip.
  • Confirmed reservation details remain on your device as part of your saved trip. Pasted source text and reservation source images are not retained after processing.
  • Current wait responses are cached in memory for up to one minute to avoid duplicate requests. Shipping version 1.0 does not save a persistent history of Queue-Times wait responses.
  • You can change or remove a member's photo from Edit Party Member, or delete the party member from the party list. Removing Park Pilot deletes its locally stored app data.
  • The Contacts and Photos pickers do not give Park Pilot ongoing access to either library. They provide only the contact or image you select, so there is no continuing Contacts or Photos permission to revoke.
  • You can revoke Location or Calendar access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security.

Children

Park Pilot does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
