Privacy Policy

Park Pilot is provided by Arcem LLC and is built to keep your trip information on your device.

What the app uses

  • Location: to show where you are in the park and calculate walking directions to your next stop. Park Pilot does not send your live location to its own servers or to its wait-time provider. Apple Maps may receive the source and destination coordinates when you request walking directions.
  • Calendar: only if you choose to import plans for your selected park day. You can review, edit, and remove imported reservations in the app. This information stays on your device.
  • Contacts: only if you choose a person with Apple's system contact picker. Park Pilot receives only the contact you select and uses the selected name, birthday-derived age, and photo to create a party member on your device.
  • Photos: only if you choose an image with Apple's system photo picker. The selected party-member photo is resized and stored on your device.
  • Wait times in the updated app: the app sends a public park identifier to ThemeParks.wiki through its anonymous live API. It sends no live location, party, reservation, contact or photo content, uses no provider account or API key, and disables request cookies. The service receives an IP address and ordinary network-request metadata. Its privacy notice states that IP addresses are briefly retained for rate limiting and abuse prevention: the general counter lasts one minute and an abuse block can last up to 30 minutes. Park Pilot does not call the provider's history API. The hosting provider separately keeps short-lived server logs; the notice does not specify a numeric duration or complete fields for those logs. The provider says this information is not used for advertising, marketing profiles or data-broker sales.
  • Earlier internal builds: the September version 1.0.0 build 1 uses Queue-Times.com instead. It sends a public park identifier, not party content or live location. That provider's website privacy policy describes network metadata and website services but does not specify API-specific log retention. Installing the updated build changes the provider; the older build is not silently relabeled.
  • Weather: the app sends the selected park's public coordinates to Apple WeatherKit to request current conditions. It does not send your live location.
  • Maps: Apple Maps provides map content for the park and viewed map area. Apple receives the network information needed to provide its services, including an IP address, under Apple's privacy policy.

What we collect

Park Pilot has no backend user accounts, custom analytics, advertising, third-party tracking code, app-generated device identifier, or partner reporting feed. Arcem LLC does not operate a server that receives your trip or usage data and does not sell your personal information. Information you enter or select is stored locally as part of your saved trip. Service requests and the network information visible to providers are described above.

If you choose to email support, we receive the email and any information or attachments you include so we can respond. Please avoid sending sensitive information or details about children.

Apple may provide the developer with aggregate App Store usage metrics and crash or diagnostic reports from people who chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS. Apple controls that system-level choice and its privacy protections. Park Pilot does not add reservation text, screenshots, party details, contact selections, photos, precise location, or route history to analytics or diagnostics, and we do not share Apple's reports with partners. You can manage Apple's choice in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

Storage, deletion, and access

  • Party-member details and selected photos remain on your device for as long as that member remains in the saved trip.
  • Reservation details remain on your device as part of your saved trip.
  • The updated app reuses successful wait responses for five minutes and respects longer provider rate-limit instructions. A bounded on-device disk cache can retain last-known waits for up to 24 hours. Cached readings are labeled cached and are not presented as live.
  • Wait observations used for local estimates are kept separately by provider, with 30-day retention, bounded reads and a 20,000-row target cap. Legacy excess rows are trimmed in batches of at most 1,000 on real readings. Old source records are not renamed as the new feed. Estimates are not guaranteed wait times.
  • Earlier build 1 uses a one-minute in-memory wait cache. Updating the app preserves saved trips and keeps old source data separate; the existing history retention policy still applies. Removing Park Pilot deletes its locally stored app data.
  • You can change or remove a member's photo from Edit Party Member, or delete the party member from the party list. Removing Park Pilot deletes its locally stored app data.
  • The Contacts and Photos pickers do not give Park Pilot ongoing access to either library. They provide only the contact or image you select, so there is no continuing Contacts or Photos permission to revoke.
  • You can revoke Location or Calendar access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security.

Children

Park Pilot helps a group leader organize a park visit. Party details, including any children's names, ages, and selected photos entered by the group leader, are stored locally and are not transmitted to Arcem LLC by the app. Please do not include children's personal information in support emails.
